ℹ️ About This App

What is this?
A lightweight, offline‑first web app for medical interns to track daily attendance, leaves, learning, and streaks. It’s designed as a fun, self‑managed tool for your batch – no accounts, no servers, no hidden costs. You own your data.

Why "Beta"?
We're testing it with a small group. Everything works, but we want your feedback to make it better. Use it, break it, suggest improvements.

Features at a glance:
✅ Check In / Check Out with real‑time rules (8:45–9:05 AM = full day; after 9:05 = half‑day; 12:30–12:45 PM = regular out‑punch).
📅 Leaves – 15 per year, half‑day consumes 0.5, full‑day absent deducts 1. Go beyond 15? Enter the "Danger Zone" with a funny message "TU YAHA PE HI REH JAYEGA!!".
🔥 Streak counter – rewards for 5, 7, 14, 30+ consecutive days.
📊 Graphs – weekly attendance, full‑day vs half‑day pie chart, cumulative attendance trend.
📝 Daily learning log – what did you learn today? Auto‑saved on check‑out.
⏳ Internship countdown – set your start date, see days remaining + progress bar.
🌙 Dark mode – easy on the eyes.
📁 Export to CSV / Import from CSV – full control over your data.
🧪 Test mode – simulate any time of day and advance days (for testing rules without waiting).

Data privacy: Everything is stored only on your device (localStorage). No one else can see your data unless you share the CSV file.

How to play together (the "game" part):
Compare streaks, leaves left, and attendance % by sharing CSV exports.
Create a friendly competition: "Who has the longest streak this month?"
Share learning logs in a group chat.
The app is a personal tracker, not a centralised leaderboard – but exporting and comparing is easy.
